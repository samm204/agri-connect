About AgriConnect

AgriConnect is an agribusiness start-up that connects smallholder farmers directly with buyers such as households, hotels, schools and exporters.

Our mission

Give small farmers a fair, simple route to market, and give buyers dependable access to fresh, traceable produce.

How we work

  • Every farmer account is reviewed and approved by our administrators.
  • Buyers see the real price, unit and stock for each listing.
  • Each order moves through Placed, Confirmed, Dispatched and Delivered, and you get a printable receipt.
